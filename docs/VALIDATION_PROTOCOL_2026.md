# NFL Player Props — Validation Protocol 2026

Protocol version: `2026.1`

## Purpose

This document defines the scientific firewall between model development,
historical evaluation, and prospective validation.

No model parameter, feature, architecture, calibration rule, prop-family
selection, or promotion decision may bypass this protocol.

## Evidence classes

### Development

Data may be used to fit, tune, diagnose, compare, or redesign the model.

Results from development data are not presented as untouched holdout evidence.

### Retrospective OOS

A scored observation must have been generated by an outer rolling-origin fold
whose complete fitting and model-selection lineage predates the observation.

This is stronger than an ordinary historical backtest, but repeated human
inspection of the same historical era still makes it retrospective evidence.

### Prospective

The model specification is frozen before outcomes occur.

No outcome from the prospective period may influence model specification,
parameters, calibration policy, eligibility rules, or exclusions.

## 2025 classification

The 2025 NFL season has already influenced model diagnosis and development.

Therefore, for any new challenger created after that inspection:

`2025 = development / retrospective audit evidence`

It must never be described as a pristine untouched holdout.

## 2026 classification

Once the final 2026 specification is frozen before regular-season outcomes:

`2026 = prospective holdout`

Every eligible prediction after freeze must be retained, including losing
predictions.

There are no post-hoc performance-based exclusions.

## Rolling-origin rule

Random train/test splitting is forbidden.

For every outer fold:

1. model fitting uses information no later than `train_end`;
2. inner model/hyperparameter selection uses outcomes available no later than
   `selection_end`;
3. `selection_end < score_start`;
4. the outer scoring interval is evaluated exactly once for that experiment;
5. later outer folds may expand their training history chronologically;
6. outer scoring intervals may not overlap.

## Point-in-time rule

Every feature, state, roster, injury observation, market quote, prior,
calibration observation, and derived input must satisfy its point-in-time
availability rule relative to the prediction timestamp.

The predicted game's result is forbidden from its own input lineage.

Closing-market information is evaluation-only for predictions made before
close.

The target prop's own price is forbidden from `p_fundamental`.

## Market benchmark rule

Model and market probabilities must be compared on the same prop, line, side,
vendor/benchmark definition, and prediction timestamp whenever the market
benchmark is used.

Missing benchmark coverage is reported; it is not silently imputed.

## Calibration rule

Calibration fitting and calibrator selection are prequential/OOF only.

A target observation cannot participate in fitting or selecting the calibrator
used to score itself.

## Experiment lineage

Every validation experiment must record at minimum:

- experiment ID;
- evidence class;
- source SHA-256;
- configuration SHA-256;
- immutable data-manifest SHA-256;
- validation-protocol version;
- explicit outer-fold chronology;
- model version;
- prediction timestamps;
- calibration cutoff;
- random seed / RNG lineage where applicable.

## Promotion

No candidate is promoted merely because a point estimate improves.

Promotion requires the predeclared Phase-10 gates, leakage checks,
simulation invariants, reproducibility requirements, adequate effective sample
size, and stability reporting.

A failed or inconclusive mandatory gate means no promotion.

## Compute policy

Laptop:
- source editing;
- contract inspection;
- small unit tests;
- lint of touched files;
- Git operations.

GitHub-hosted compute:
- model fitting;
- parameter searches;
- historical simulation;
- full walk-forward evaluation;
- bootstrap inference;
- reproducibility rebuilds;
- other sustained CPU workloads.

## Scientific interpretation

Historical evidence can support model development and risk reduction.

Only genuinely future predictions produced after model freeze can provide
untouched prospective evidence for the final 2026 model.
